                            Solutions
• Simply use large devices
   – For each extra bit
       • Reduce offset by 2x → increase device width by 4x
       • Increase comparator number by 2x
       • Overall 8x in power
• Input/output series offset cancellation
   – Reduce speed
• Offset averaging
• Calibration
• Comparator redundancy
